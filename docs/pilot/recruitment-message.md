# Dex Lens pilot recruitment messages

## Slack or WhatsApp

Hey — I’m inviting 6–8 people to try an early test of Dex Lens, a local tool
for people who use Claude Code on a Mac. It looks at the setup you already use
for a repeated job and gives a plain-English second opinion: what it appears
able to do, what’s backed by evidence, and where the picture is still
uncertain. You don’t need Dex, and nothing is changed in this first diagnosis.
Taking part is completely voluntary; we’ll ask before reading a folder you
choose. If a useful improvement comes up, it would be optional, previewed
first, and reversible. Do you use Claude Code most weeks and have a job you
repeat with it? If yes and you’d be curious, reply here and I’ll share details.

## Email

**Subject: Invitation: small Dex Lens pilot for Claude Code users**

Hi [Name],

I’m putting together a small, voluntary pilot of Dex Lens and looking for 6–8
people who use Claude Code on a Mac most weeks and have at least one real job
they repeat with it. Most participants will be people who don’t use Dex.

Dex Lens is an early, local tool that gives your existing personal AI setup a
second opinion. You choose the setup folder to inspect; it reads that selected
folder and organises its findings around the job you say matters. It shows what
your setup appears able to do, what is supported by evidence, and where the
evidence is weak or still unknown. It is not a score, a sales funnel, or a
request to replace your system.

The first diagnosis is read-only: it does not change files, and it runs on your
Mac. There’s no account to create and no analytics. Taking part is optional,
and you can stop at any point. If we see a possible improvement, it would be
offered separately, shown in full before action, and reversible — you decide
whether anything happens.

This is early testing, so I’m looking for honest reactions as much as positive
results. Interested? Reply and I’ll send the short participant details.

Best,

Dave

## If they ask what they would do or whether their data is safe

You’d choose one Claude Code setup folder for the initial diagnosis, confirm
the job you want it to look at, and talk me through whether the findings match
reality. The first pass is read-only: Dex Lens runs locally on your Mac, reads
only the folder you approve, and does not edit files, contact services, or
require an account. You can stop or withdraw at any time. We won’t share
anything from your setup unless we ask separately and you agree. If a possible
improvement is suggested, you’ll see exactly what it would do before deciding;
it would be optional and reversible. It’s early testing, so the main ask is
your time and candid feedback.

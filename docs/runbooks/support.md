# Support runbook

**Trigger:** a participant reports confusion, a defect, or an access issue.

**Owner:** pilot support owner.

**Actions:** acknowledge without asking for raw private content; triage using
references and bounded summaries; record a safe resolution or escalation.

**Evidence:** support case, safe reproduction summary, and resolution note.

**Exit criteria:** the case is resolved or escalated, and no unapproved data
was collected during support.

**Tabletop:** the deterministic M6 drill uses a synthetic support request and
records the no-raw-content path.
